🎓 How to Imitate a Qari

1. Listen in very short phrases

Do not chase the whole āyah at once. Hear a short phrase 2–3 times until you can predict the Qari's rise, fall and pause.

2. Copy the shape, not the physical voice

Imitate pitch movement, rhythm, pauses and emotional flow. Do not strain the throat trying to sound physically identical to the reciter.

3. Shadow immediately

Play the phrase and repeat almost immediately after it. The shorter the delay, the easier it is for the ear to retain the sound.

4. Slow down when necessary

Use 0.8× while learning a difficult phrase, then return to normal speed so the child learns the natural flow.

5. Record and A/B compare

Qari → child → Qari → child. Ask the child to identify where their pitch, pause or rhythm changed.

Golden Rule

Correct recitation first → beautiful voice second. Melody must never change a letter, madd, ghunnah, waqf or Tajweed rule.
